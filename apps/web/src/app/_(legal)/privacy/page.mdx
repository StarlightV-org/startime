# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18.08.2026 - 10:32_

Star Time ([time.starlightv.dev](https://time.starlightv.dev)) is a project operated by a small team of developers.
This page describes what data we collect in our Service, what we do with it, and how you can manage your data.

## §1. Data we collect

### §1.1. Account Information

When signing up with GitHub, we receive information about your account, like your username, email, and profile picture. We never receive password information.

### §1.2. Coding Activities

The editor plugins send your activity data to the server. The following data is sent:

- `eventTime`: The time the event occurred.
- `language`: The programming language used.
- `project`: The project name.
- `fileHash`: The hash of the file. (will be hashed again on the server)
- `editor`: The editor used. (e.g. Visual Studio Code, Zed, Cursor)
- `platform`: The platform used. (e.g. Windows 11, macOS 26)

> [!important]
> Your source code is never sent to the server.

### §1.3. Server Logs

When you use the Service, we may log your requests and server responses. This is used for debugging and improving the Service. These logs are stored for less than 30 days.
Log entries include:

- `requestTime`: The time the request was received.
- `method`: The HTTP method used.
- `url`: The URL of the request.
- `status`: The HTTP status code of the response.
- `duration`: The time taken to process the request.
- `ip`: The IP address of the client.

### §1.4. Analytics

We host our own instance of [OpenPannel](https://openpanel.dev/) and collect user activity on our server and on the client.
This data is used to improve the Service and provide usage metrics to our users.

## §2. What we do with your data

### §2.1. Data Usage

- Aggregate your coding activity to Render your Overview
- Compile it for Data Exports
- Rendering Leaderboards and activity overviews in your organization
- Rendering Public Leaderboards

### §2.2. Data Visibility

Default: Your data is Private, not other users can see it.
In Organizations: Only other members of your organization can see your data.
Public: Your data is visible to other signed-in users and is listed on the public leaderboard.

### §2.3. Data Sharing

- We do not sell your data to third parties.
- We do not share your data with Advertisers.
- We do not use your data for training large language models.

### §2.4. Third-Party

Your data is processed by third parties as needed:

- GitHub: OAuth signing
- Hosting Provider: Runs the Server and the Database

Our Service is hosted in a Data Center in Frankfurt, Germany.
All data is processed and stored on that server.
